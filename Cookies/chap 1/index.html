Chocolate Chip Cookies

These classic chocolate chip cookies are crispy on the edges and chewy in the center — perfect for any occasion!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Method

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cream together the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla.
  4. Combine the flour, baking soda, and salt; stir into the creamed mixture.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Drop by spoonfuls onto ungreased baking sheets.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
  8. Cool on wire racks and enjoy!
